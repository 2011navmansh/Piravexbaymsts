Great Northern Corridor’s Bhagalpur section. Bhagalpur Junction, Sultanganj, Kahalgaon and Vikramshila on the Sahibganj loop. 4 split RAR parts, about 7.95 GB. Download all four and extract Part 1. 1 Nov 2026.

Install

Four steps. Then pick the route.

01

Download and extract

Grab the ZIP or RAR and extract it with WinRAR or 7-Zip.

02

Copy to Routes

Move the route folder into Train Simulator/ROUTES/.

03

Run installme.bat

Double-click installme.bat in the route folder so the default assets copy in.

04

Launch

Open MSTS or Open Rails, select the new route, and start the session.

Split packs need every part. Where a route page says so, extract Part 1 or Part 01 after the full set is downloaded.
