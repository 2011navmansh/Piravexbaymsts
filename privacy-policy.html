01 · Collection

Information we do collect

Piravexbay stores and collects the sign-up data during registration — for example, your username and email. To keep personal details safe, users are encouraged to create accounts with fake data, except for the email address, which is needed for password recovery.

We do not collect any payment information, because all services on Piravexbay are free. Whenever you register, use fake details except for your email for password recovery.

02 · Cookies

Cookies and tracking

We use a small number of cookies that are strictly necessary to run the site: a session cookie, a security token, and your signed-in state. We do not use them to track your browsing behaviour across other websites. Theme preferences are kept in your browser's localStorage.

Some pages carry a small amount of advertising from a third-party advertising network that helps cover server costs. It may set cookies or collect technical data such as your IP address and device type under its own privacy policy. Members with an active subscription are not shown these ads.

External websites that we link to may also use cookies or other tracking mechanisms. Review the privacy policies of any third-party sites you visit from Piravexbay.

03 · Links

Third-party links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. Piravexbay is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these external sites. When you follow a link to a third-party site, you are subject to their privacy policies and terms. Review those policies before engaging with their content.

04 · Security

Data security

Since Piravexbay does not collect any personal data, we do not store, manage, or protect any user information. Users can download content from the site without providing any personal details.

05 · Children

Children's privacy

Piravexbay is not directed towards individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal data from children. If we discover that any personal data has been collected from a child without verification of parental consent, we will delete such information immediately.

06 · Changes

Changes to this privacy policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page, with the date of the last update clearly displayed at the top. Review it periodically to stay informed.

07 · Contact
