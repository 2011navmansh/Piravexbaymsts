The set

NZM, Kota, Mumbai Central.

The pack covers premium day trains, long-haul mail expresses and weekly superfast runs through Hazrat Nizamuddin, Kota, Nagda, Ratlam, Vadodara and Mumbai Central.

  • Flagship day trains: 12002 Shatabdi, 20171 and 20172 Vande Bharat, Garib Rath and Rajdhani.

  • Weekly SF, intercity, overnight and double-duty workings.
