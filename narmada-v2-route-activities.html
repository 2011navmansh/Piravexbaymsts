The set

Itarsi to Satna.

Four ready-to-run scenario files for the Narmada V2 route in Microsoft Train Simulator and Open Rails. The set covers long-distance express runs across the Narmada valley mainline.

The run moves from the busy approaches around Itarsi through Jabalpur and onward toward Katni and Satna.

Each activity is one RAR. Download the scenario you want, extract it, and copy the activity files into the ACTIVITIES folder of the Narmada V2 route.

  • 11072 — Kamayani Express — 88 KB.
  • 11077 — Jhelum Express — 71 KB.
  • 12628 — Karnataka Express (PT) — 73 KB.
  • 12943 — Udyogkarmi SF Express (PT) — 70 KB.
  • Requires the Narmada V2 route and its track packs.
