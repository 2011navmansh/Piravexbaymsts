Make a horn

Five steps. Then the sim plays it.

01

Download and install

Grab the archive, extract it, and install REAPER on the PC.

02

Get a horn sample

Record a clean loco horn clip, or reuse a free WAV, and import it into REAPER.

03

Clean and loop

Trim noise at the edges. Find a seamless loop on the sustain and play it until it sounds continuous.

04

Render

Render the looped section as a plain 44.1 kHz 16-bit WAV, named exactly like the loco horn file.

05

Drop into MSTS

Replace the horn WAV in TRAINS/TRAINSET/<loco>/SOUND, or the carrier SOUND folder, then open MSTS or Open Rails.

MSTS loco files reference horn WAVs by name in the eng and wag sound blocks. Keep the file names identical so the sim picks up the new loop horn. This is one archived package. Works on Windows x64. Do not rearrange the extracted folder.
