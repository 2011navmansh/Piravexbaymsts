The corridor

Secunderabad, Guntakal, Hubli.

The Dakshina Madhya Railway is a recreation of the South Central Railway trunk across the Deccan plateau. SCR was established in October 1966 as India's ninth railway zone, headquartered at Secunderabad Junction, formed from the Hubli and Vijayawada divisions of Southern Railway and the Solapur and Secunderabad divisions of Central Railway. The zone covers 3,127 km of track across 883 stations.

  • Secunderabad — major SCR hub and the route's starting point, home to LF push-pull express operations.
  • Guntakal Junction — where several routes meet, known for loco changeovers and yard activity.
  • Hubli Junction — gateway to North Karnataka, now in the South Western Railway zone, a freight and passenger hub.
  • Deccan plateau — undulating ground, viaducts, tunnels and gradients.
