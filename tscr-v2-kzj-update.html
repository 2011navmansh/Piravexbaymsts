The junction

Delhi–Chennai, through KZJ.

The TSCR-V2-KZJ Update is the V2 refresh of the TSCR route, centred on Kazipet Junction. The update rebuilds the junction and its approaches with station detail, fresh trackwork and a scenery and texture pass.

  • Kazipet Junction (KZJ) — platform, yard and signal-cabin detail on the SCR crossroads.
  • Updated trackwork — refined track, turnouts and block sections across the junction and its approaches.
  • Fresh scenery and textures along the SCR mainline.
  • Long-distance expresses, suburban workings and mixed freight.
  • Built for Microsoft Train Simulator and Open Rails on Windows.
Install

Four steps. Then drive.

01

Download and extract

Download all 6 RAR parts into one folder, then extract Part 1 with WinRAR or 7-Zip.

02

Copy to Routes

Move the extracted route folder into .../Train Simulator/ROUTES/.

03

Run installme.bat

Double-click installme.bat inside the route folder if it is included.

04

Launch

Open MSTS or Open Rails, select the TSCR route, and start the session.
