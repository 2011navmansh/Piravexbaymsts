01 · Notification

Copyright infringement notification

If you believe that your copyrighted work has been used or distributed on Piravexbay without your permission, submit a DMCA takedown notice. The notice must include:

  1. Identification of the copyrighted work: a clear description of the work you claim has been infringed, including any relevant registration numbers.
  2. Location of infringing material: the URL or specific location on this site. Give enough detail that the file can be found quickly.
  3. Your contact information: name, address, telephone number, and email address.
  4. Statement of good faith: a statement that you have a good faith belief the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  5. Statement of accuracy: a statement that the information is accurate, and under penalty of perjury, that you are the owner or authorized to act for the owner.
  6. Your signature: a physical or electronic signature of the owner or a person authorized to act for them.

Send the notice to br46plw06@mozmail.com.

02 · Response

Response to notifications

Upon receiving a valid DMCA takedown notice, we will:

  1. Remove or disable access to the infringing content.
  2. Notify the person who shared the material of the removal and provide details of the claim.
  3. If they believe the content was removed by mistake, they may submit a counter-notification.
03 · Counter

Counter-notification

If you believe your content was wrongly removed because of a mistake or misidentification, you may file a counter-notification. It must include:

  1. Identification of the removed content: a clear description of the material and the URL where it appeared before removal.
  2. Your contact information: name, address, telephone number, and email address.
  3. Statement of good faith: a statement that you believe, in good faith, the material was removed by mistake or misidentification.
  4. Consent to jurisdiction: a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the relevant court in your district and agree to accept service of process from the person who filed the original notice.
  5. Your signature: a physical or electronic signature.

Send the counter-notification to br46plw06@mozmail.com.

After a counter-notification, the removed content may be restored unless the copyright owner files a lawsuit seeking a court order to prevent restoration.

04 · Repeat

Repeat infringers

Piravexbay takes copyright infringement seriously. In accordance with the DMCA and other applicable laws, we will terminate access or block repeat infringers and remove content that is found to repeatedly violate copyright.
