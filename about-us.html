Our story

Born from bad mirrors.

Redefining game and MSTS downloads — built because sketchy mirrors and endless walls were the only other option.

Our story, since 2026: born from the frustration of sketchy mirrors and endless download walls — built into the clean, fast, totally free bay we always wanted.

Hey there, fellow gamers. Pull up a chair — this is Piravexbay. It started with the story every one of us knows.

Picture this: it's 2026, and we're huddled around a computer, cursing at yet another sketchy download that's taking forever — and is probably packed with more junk than a hospital ward. Sound familiar?

Every train-sim enthusiast we know had the same version of that story: hunting for a single Indian MSTS route across ten sketchy mirrors, half of them broken, the rest demanding endless steps to reach one file.

That's when we decided to build the platform we'd always dreamed of — a place where downloading games and MSTS addons doesn't feel like navigating a minefield while wearing a blindfold.

Fast forward to 2026: Piravexbay was born. Today it's home to pre-installed games and a growing, hand-picked library of Indian MSTS routes, locomotives, sounds and mods — all in one clean, fast and totally free bay.

Welcome to Piravexbay. Game on, friends.

The promise

What's our deal?

Five things every download on the bay has to live up to.

01

Testing and verification

Every route, loco, sound pack and game is manually tested and scanned before it goes live, and we only source from uploaders we trust. These checks are not a guarantee that a file is malware-free — keep your antivirus enabled and tell us if something looks off.

02

A download that doesn't fight you

Remember the last download that made you want to throw your PC out the window? No pop-up mazes, no waiting rooms, no "step 3 of 12" nonsense here — one click, full speed, done.

03

Free as a bird

Everything on the bay is 100% free. No catches, no subscriptions, no "oops, you need to upgrade" surprises. Awesome gaming shouldn't cost an arm and a leg.

04

Portable and pre-installed

A ton of our games are portable, plug-and-play and ready to run — perfect for gaming on the go, or when you just can't be bothered with installations.

05

Built for the MSTS community

It's not just PC games — Indian Railway routes, authentic locomotives, realistic sounds and mods, all ready for Microsoft Train Simulator and Open Rails.
